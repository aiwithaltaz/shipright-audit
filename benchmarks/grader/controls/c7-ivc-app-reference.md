# Review: event registration desk app

Verdict: the desk works for simple cases, but lookup, family handling and missing requirements slow volunteers down. Fix lookup and the per-person next step first.

## What works (keep)

1. Regional context and scoped counts are useful; keep them.
2. The registrations list, the View dialog and the ticket preview are clean; keep them.
3. The reports structure works; keep it.

## Problems

1. The oversized sidebar logo and heavy title compete with the work; shrink the branding on this work screen.
2. Date, time, venue, fees and shirt info are repeated and always take space on the desk screen.
3. The "Before you begin" panel adds little; move its rules into context where they apply.
4. Lookup works only by phone, but phones can be wrong, unknown or shared; add name lookup and combinations.
5. A 17-year-old only learns about the parent signature late; show prerequisites like the parent signature at lookup time.
6. A blocked requirement (Code of Conduct) shows no fix; put the fixing action right on the blocked state.
7. Staff may see proof on the attendee's phone; let staff mark it complete with an audit of who and when.
8. Inactive volunteers need facilitator evidence; this is a policy decision to confirm, with what evidence is needed.
9. Adults also need a waiver, but the flow skips the adult waiver; ask about it or add it.
10. The side card should be a live check of the details being entered, not a static summary.
11. Starting from a child needs a way to add or link a guardian inline without going back.
12. When a volunteer parent is found, show their linked children.
13. One guardian can cover several children and may also attend; handle one guardian across children and as an attendee.
14. Family progress must show each person (done, current, needs attention) and let staff revisit anyone.
15. If the parent is not present there is no usable path; offer send a link, capture contact, or sign later with a pending state.
16. Money tendered is not money kept; show change due and never turn change into a donation automatically.
17. Someone may want to donate without registering; add a donation-only flow.
18. A pending payment should show at search with the amount due and a Collect payment action.
19. Every missing condition needs the same direct recovery: show all missing items and the next fix per person.
20. Record who did what and when (creation, verification, payment) as an audit trail.

## Fix first

Add name lookup, surface every missing requirement with its fix on the search result, and handle families per person.
